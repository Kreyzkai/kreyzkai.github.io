Printing Tips
  1. Click Print Poster or press Ctrl/⌘+P.
  2. Set scale to 100% / Actual size — never “Fit to page”, or tiles won't match.
  3. Margins: None, and enable background graphics in your browser dialog.
  4. Pick paper matching the size chosen here (e.g. A4), portrait/landscape as shown.
  5. Trim each sheet on the dashed guide, match the corner letters (A1, B2…) and tape from behind.
