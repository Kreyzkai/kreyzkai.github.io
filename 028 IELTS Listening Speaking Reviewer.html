🗺️ Your IELTS Test Day / テスト当日の流れ

Know the journey, and nothing can surprise you. 🎯 = rooms this reviewer trains.

1 BOOK → 2 CHECK IN → 3 🎯 LISTENING → 4 READING → 5 WRITING → 6 🎯 SPEAKING → 7 SCORES

Listening → Reading → Writing always run together (about 2 h 40 on paper, about 2 h 30 on computer, no breaks). Speaking may be before/after, or on a different day up to 7 days before/after — your booking confirmation says when.

1

📝 Book your test

Choose Academic or General Training, and paper or computer at a test centre. Listening and Speaking are the same for both; Reading and Writing are different. Your confirmation gives the date, time, place, and your Speaking time.

申し込み時に種類と方法を選びます。

2

🎒 Pack & arrive early

Bring the same ID you used when you booked. Into the test room: only your ID and water in a clear bottle with no label. Phones, watches, electronics and your own pens stay outside. The centre takes your photo (some centres also scan a finger). Arrive with plenty of time — if you are late, you may not be able to take the test. Eat and use the toilet before: there are no breaks.

持ち物は身分証と透明な水筒だけ。早めに到着しましょう。

3

🪪 Check in

Staff check your ID, take your photo, store your belongings, and show you your seat. An invigilator explains the rules. This is the same for everyone — it is not a test. Need help? Raise your hand quietly.

受付は全員同じ手続き。困ったら静かに手を挙げましょう。

4

🎧 Listening room 🎯 — about 30 min, 40 questions

First test of the day. The sound is checked — if you cannot hear clearly, raise your hand before it starts. You hear 4 recordings, each ONE time only, getting harder (chat → talk → study talk → lecture). Before each part there is a short moment to read the questions: that is your prediction time (Listening lessons train exactly this). Write as you listen; paper tests get transfer time at the end, computer tests a short check time.

Listening は最初・1回だけ再生。予想する時間がカギです。

5

📖✍️ Reading (60 min, 40 questions) → Writing (60 min, 2 tasks)

No break between them. Reading answers go straight onto the answer sheet/screen (no extra transfer time). A clock is in the room. This reviewer gives you an overview of both — full training needs timed practice tests too.

休憩なしで続きます。時計を見ながら進めましょう。

6

🗣️ Speaking room 🎯 — 11–14 min, one examiner

Show the same ID and wait to be called. Part 1 (4–5 min) questions about you → Part 2 topic card, 1 minute of keyword notes, speak 1–2 minutes → Part 3 (4–5 min) deeper discussion. Your speaking is recorded (normal for everyone). The examiner controls the time — being stopped does not mean you failed — and will not tell you your score.

試験官が時間を管理します。止められても失敗ではありません。

7

🏁 Finish & scores

Stay seated until staff say you can leave, collect your belongings, and report any problem on the day. Later you receive 4 scores + 1 overall score (1–9) on your Test Report Form; when and how depends on your test type and centre.

結果は4技能＋総合スコア（1〜9）で届きます。

🎧 Lesson 3 · Trick answers — don't stop at the first answer 6 MIN · PRINT MISSION 3
⚠️ The first answer is often a trick! Listen for: but · however · instead · changed to

🎧 Listen and answer. 聞いて答えましょう（答えのあとでスクリプトを見てもOK）

1. Which day will Maya attend?
2. Where should visitors meet?
3. How much is the final fee?
4. What must learners bring?
📄 Script — open AFTER you answer

Maya first booked the Monday session, but her work schedule changed, so she will attend on Wednesday instead. Visitors used to gather at reception; however, building work means everyone should now meet at the library entrance. The standard fee is fifty pounds, although early registration reduces it to thirty-five pounds. Learners do not need a laptop or headphones, but they must bring a notebook.

13 or 30? Listen to the STRESS / 強く読む場所を聞こう
thirTEEN (13) = strong at the end ↗  |  THIRty (30) = strong at the start ↘

🎧 Listen. Write these 6. 聞いて書こう（何回聞いてもOK）

✏️ Spelling trick: LOOK 👀 → COVER 🙈 → WRITE ✍️ → CHECK ✓
見て・隠して・書いて・確認。間違えたら3回書こう。

When you make a mistake, ask why / 間違えた理由

A 🎧 I didn't hear it · P 🔮 My guess was wrong · D ⚠️ Trick answer · S ✏️ Spelling · F 💤 I lost focus. Knowing the reason tells you what to practise next — here, or in a full practice test (this reviewer trains 2 of the many Listening question types).

🗣️ Speaking / スピーキング

11–14 minutes · you + one examiner · recorded · three parts. Judged equally on fluency, vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation — being easy to understand matters more than any accent.

🗣️ Part 1 · Answer + reason + example 4–5 MIN IN TEST · PRINT MISSION 5
Don't answer with one word. Use 3 steps:
1語で終わらせず、3ステップで少し長く答えよう。
① ANSWERYes, I do.
② REASONbecause…
③ EXAMPLEFor example…
Say it — 30 seconds
00:30
🎙 Record yourself / 録音してみよう（端末の中だけ・アップロードされません）
Recordings stay on your device and are not saved by this site.
📄 Models — open AFTER you try
Starter

Yes, I do. I like English because I can talk to more people. For example, I can use it when I travel.

Step up · try ONE new phrase

Yes, I do, especially when I can use it in real conversations. When I travelled last year, I was able to ask for directions instead of relying on a translation app.

In the real test you speak for 1–2 minutes. When 1 minute feels easy, try 2. 本番は1〜2分。慣れたら2分に挑戦。 More cue cards: a useful lesson · a place you enjoyed · something you bought · a person who helped you.

🎙 Record yourself
Listen once for ideas, once for English.
📄 Models — open AFTER you try
Starter · about 45 seconds

I would like to learn how to play the guitar. I want to learn it because I enjoy music. I would take online lessons and practise for twenty minutes every day. For example, I could learn one easy song first. I would feel proud if I could play it well. Overall, it would help me relax.

Step up · about 90 seconds · try ONE new phrase

To begin with, a skill I would really like to learn is playing the guitar. One reason I want to learn it is that music helps me relax, and it would be a good way to spend time with friends. I would probably start with short online lessons, because they are flexible, and I would practise for about twenty minutes a day so that I improve gradually. A good example is my cousin, who learned in this way. What would help most is being patient. Overall, it is a skill I could enjoy for the rest of my life.

🗣️ Part 3 · Opinion + reason + example + qualify 4–5 MIN IN TEST
Go deeper than Part 1: Opinion → Reason → Example → “That said…”
意見だけでなく、理由・例・反対の見方も言いましょう。
Say it — about 1 minute
01:00
📄 Models — open AFTER you try
Starter

I think online courses can be good. This is because students can study at any time. For example, they can watch a lesson again. But some students learn better with a teacher in a classroom.

Step up · try ONE new phrase

In my opinion, online courses can be just as effective as classroom lessons, but it depends on the learner. This is because online study is flexible: students can replay difficult parts and learn at their own pace. For example, a student who works during the day can study late in the evening. That said, classroom lessons offer immediate feedback and more interaction, so younger or less self-disciplined learners may make faster progress with a teacher in front of them.

“Let me think about that…” · “What I mean is…” · “It is a kind of…” · “It is something you use to…” · “Please say that again.” — being stuck is OK. Keep talking.

📖 Reading / リーディング

60 minutes · 40 questions · right after Listening, with no break. This segment is an overview + starter practice — full training needs timed practice tests.

Academic: 3 long passages (books, journals, magazines) with 40 questions in total. General Training: shorter everyday/work texts plus longer passages. Question types: multiple choice, True/False/Not Given, matching headings/information, sentence & note completion, and more. No extra transfer time — write answers on the answer sheet/screen as you go.
60分・40問。答えはその場で解答用紙に書きます。

5 strategies that help most

1 · Questions first

Read the questions before the passage. You read with a purpose.

2 · Hunt names & numbers

Capital letters, dates and numbers are easy to find — start there.

3 · Same meaning again

Answers are paraphrased, exactly like Listening Lesson 2.

4 · ~20 minutes per passage

Don't get stuck. Mark it, move on, come back.

5 · Never leave a blank

No minus points for wrong answers. Guess!

True / False / Not Given

FALSE = the text says the opposite. NOT GIVEN = the text doesn't say. Don't use your own knowledge.
📖 Starter practice — The Bike Library 5 MIN
The Bike Library
Riverton's Bike Library opened in 2023 in a former ticket office next to the central station. For £15 a year, members can borrow a bicycle for up to seven days at a time, and a helmet is included free. The library owns 120 bicycles, most of them donated by local residents. Volunteers check the brakes and tyres every week, and on the first Saturday of each month they run a free repair workshop where members can learn to fix common problems. The library is open from Tuesday to Saturday. Its manager says the most popular bicycles are the small city bikes, because most members ride only two or three kilometres to school or work.

Questions 1–4: the words in the passage are bold this time. In the real test they will not be — your job is to find them. Notice how the questions use different words (“included free” / “borrow”) from your first reading.

✍️ Writing / ライティング

60 minutes · 2 tasks · Task 2 counts about twice as much as Task 1. Overview + starter practice.

Task 1 (at least 150 words)

Academic: describe a chart, graph, table, diagram or process in your own words.
General Training: write a letter for a given situation.

Task 2 (at least 250 words)

An essay: give your opinion, discuss a problem, or argue a point. Same for Academic and General Training. Spend about 40 minutes here.
Judged on: answering the task · organising ideas clearly · vocabulary · grammar range & accuracy. A clear plan beats beautiful sentences with no structure.
構成（パラグラフ）が大事です。書く前に計画しましょう。

The 5-step routine

1 READWhat exactly is asked?
2 PLAN5 min · ideas in order
3 WRITEparagraphs!
4 COUNT150 / 250 words
5 CHECKverbs · plurals · spelling
✍️ Starter practice — plan a Task 2 essay 7 MIN
Task 2: “Are online courses as effective as classroom lessons? Discuss both views and give your own opinion.”
📄 Model paragraph — open AFTER your plan
Starter paragraph

Online courses have clear advantages. Students can study at any time and watch a lesson again if it is difficult. For example, a person who works during the day can study late in the evening. This flexibility helps many learners continue studying.

Pattern: topic sentence → reason → example → result. Four sentences, one idea.

Step up · try ONE upgrade

Online courses offer considerable flexibility, as learners can revisit difficult material and progress at their own pace; a student with a daytime job, for instance, may study late in the evening. That said, this independence only benefits learners who are sufficiently self-disciplined.

Upgrades to borrow (one!): a precise word (considerable, revisit) · “as / for instance / That said” linking · a longer sentence.

Before you finish — check

Nobody can mark your essay on this page — show it to a teacher, or compare it honestly with the model paragraph.
