🗺️ Your IELTS Test Day / テスト当日の流れ

Know the journey, and nothing can surprise you. 🎯 = rooms this reviewer trains.

1 BOOK → 2 CHECK IN → 3 🎯 LISTENING → 4 READING → 5 WRITING → 6 🎯 SPEAKING → 7 SCORES

Listening → Reading → Writing always run together (about 2 h 40 on paper, about 2 h 30 on computer, no breaks). Speaking may be before/after, or on a different day up to 7 days before/after — your booking confirmation says when.

1

📝 Book your test

Choose Academic or General Training, and paper or computer at a test centre. Listening and Speaking are the same for both; Reading and Writing are different. Your confirmation gives the date, time, place, and your Speaking time.

申し込み時に種類と方法を選びます。

2

🎒 Pack & arrive early

Bring the same ID you used when you booked. Into the test room: only your ID and water in a clear bottle with no label. Phones, watches, electronics and your own pens stay outside. The centre takes your photo (some centres also scan a finger). Arrive with plenty of time — if you are late, you may not be able to take the test. Eat and use the toilet before: there are no breaks.

持ち物は身分証と透明な水筒だけ。早めに到着しましょう。

3

🪪 Check in

Staff check your ID, take your photo, store your belongings, and show you your seat. An invigilator explains the rules. This is the same for everyone — it is not a test. Need help? Raise your hand quietly.

受付は全員同じ手続き。困ったら静かに手を挙げましょう。

4

🎧 Listening room 🎯 — about 30 min, 40 questions

First test of the day. The sound is checked — if you cannot hear clearly, raise your hand before it starts. You hear 4 recordings, each ONE time only, getting harder (chat → talk → study talk → lecture). Before each part there is a short moment to read the questions: that is your prediction time (Listening lessons train exactly this). Write as you listen; paper tests get transfer time at the end, computer tests a short check time.

Listening は最初・1回だけ再生。予想する時間がカギです。

5

📖✍️ Reading (60 min, 40 questions) → Writing (60 min, 2 tasks)

No break between them. Reading answers go straight onto the answer sheet/screen (no extra transfer time). A clock is in the room. This reviewer gives you an overview of both — full training needs timed practice tests too.

休憩なしで続きます。時計を見ながら進めましょう。

6

🗣️ Speaking room 🎯 — 11–14 min, one examiner

Show the same ID and wait to be called. Part 1 (4–5 min) questions about you → Part 2 topic card, 1 minute of keyword notes, speak 1–2 minutes → Part 3 (4–5 min) deeper discussion. Your speaking is recorded (normal for everyone). The examiner controls the time — being stopped does not mean you failed — and will not tell you your score.

試験官が時間を管理します。止められても失敗ではありません。

7

🏁 Finish & scores

Stay seated until staff say you can leave, collect your belongings, and report any problem on the day. Later you receive 4 scores + 1 overall score (1–9) on your Test Report Form; when and how depends on your test type and centre.

結果は4技能＋総合スコア（1〜9）で届きます。

📖 Reading / リーディング

60 minutes · 40 questions · right after Listening, with no break. This segment is an overview + starter practice — full training needs timed practice tests.

Academic: 3 long passages (books, journals, magazines) with 40 questions in total. General Training: shorter everyday/work texts plus longer passages. Question types: multiple choice, True/False/Not Given, matching headings/information, sentence & note completion, and more. No extra transfer time — write answers on the answer sheet/screen as you go.
60分・40問。答えはその場で解答用紙に書きます。

5 strategies that help most

1 · Questions first

Read the questions before the passage. You read with a purpose.

2 · Hunt names & numbers

Capital letters, dates and numbers are easy to find — start there.

3 · Same meaning again

Answers are paraphrased, exactly like Listening Lesson 2.

4 · ~20 minutes per passage

Don't get stuck. Mark it, move on, come back.

5 · Never leave a blank

No minus points for wrong answers. Guess!

True / False / Not Given

FALSE = the text says the opposite. NOT GIVEN = the text doesn't say. Don't use your own knowledge.

🏷️ Lesson 1 · Parts of Speech — Hinshi (品詞) 5 MIN · OPTIONAL
Every word in an English sentence has a job (its part of speech / 品詞・ひんし). Connect the Japanese grammar words you already know to their English names so IELTS tips make instant sense!
日本語で習った「品詞」と英語の名前をつなげると、IELTSのコツが一気に分かりやすくなります。

🇯🇵 ↔ 🇬🇧 3 crucial differences every Japanese learner should know:
  • Grammar boxes don't match 1-to-1: Japanese and English categories do not line up perfectly, so word-by-word translation causes mistakes.
  • Articles (Kanshi / 冠詞: a, an, the) don't exist in Japanese: that is why forgetting a / an / the is the #1 habit to watch in IELTS Writing, Speaking, and note completion!
  • Japanese particles (は・が・を) ≠ English prepositions: in Japanese, は / が / を tell you who did what. In English, position around the verb decides Subject vs Object (I play soccer, never I soccer play), and prepositions (in, on, at) go in front of the noun (in Tokyo).

IELTS Suffix Radar — guess the part of speech from the ending! / 語尾で品詞を見抜く裏ワザ

Even if you meet an unknown word in IELTS Reading, its ending usually reveals its job:

🏛️ Noun endings (Meishi)

-tion / -sion (education) · -ment (improvement) · -ness (happiness) · -ity (flexibility)

🎨 Adjective endings (Keiyōshi)

-able / -ible (affordable, flexible) · -ful (useful) · -ive (effective) · -al (practical)

⚡ Verb endings (Dōshi)

-ise / -ize (organise) · -ate (communicate) · -ify (identify) · -en (widen)

🏃 Adverb endings (Fukushi)

-ly (slowly, gradually, sharply) — plus short adverbs often, very, fast, hard.

Step B · All 5 Core Patterns + 3 Useful Extensions / 5文型＋3つの広げ方

Pattern letters: S = Subject (主語: who/what the sentence is about) · V = Verb (動詞: action or state) · O = Object (目的語: person/thing affected) · C = Complement (補語: describes/identifies S or O) · IO = Indirect Object (間接目的語: person receiving) · DO = Direct Object (直接目的語: thing given/sent).

1. S + VSubject + VerbBirds fly.Prices rose. (Task 1 chart)
2. S + V + OSubject + Verb + ObjectI like music.Technology improves education.

🛠️ Lesson 4 · Top 5 Japanese→English Traps Examiners Notice Immediately 5 MIN · OPTIONAL
🎯 Instant score boost: Fixing just these 5 high-frequency habits cleans up over half the grammar slips examiners hear and read from Japanese test-takers!
日本の受験生が一番やりがちな5つのミスです。これを知るだけで減点が一気に減ります！

1 · Double verb (be + base verb)

✗ I am play soccer.
✓ I play soccer.

Use be before a noun/adjective (I am happy), not before a regular base action verb.

2 · “I am agree” trap (Dōshi vs Keiyōshi)

✗ I am agree with this view.
✓ I agree with this view.

agree, happen, belong, depend are Verbs, not adjectives — never put am / is / are in front!

3 · Naked singular noun (missing Kanshi)

✗ Student needs notebook.
✓ A student needs a notebook. (or Students need notebooks.)

A countable singular noun never stands naked — add a / an / the / my or make it plural (-s)!

4 · Missing subject It / They (Daimeishi)

✗ Because is convenient.
✓ Because it is convenient.

Japanese omits それ, but every English clause must have a Subject (S + V) — even after because!

5 · Particle (を・について) trap

✗ discuss about a problem / listen music
✓ discuss a problem / listen to music

discuss takes an Object directly (S + V + O); listen needs the preposition to!

Bonus · Lone “Because…” fragment

✗ I like online courses. Because I can save time.
✓ I like online courses because I can save time.

In IELTS Writing, attach a because / although clause to a main clause with no period between them!

Your exam-day game plan / 4技能すべてで使える得点アップ術

🎧 Listening

In your 20–30 s preview time, pencil N (noun), Adj, V, or Num next to every gap. Check “a” vs “an” before the blank — an ________ guarantees a vowel sound!

📖 Reading

In note/summary completion, your word must fit the grammar of the summary sentence. Use core S + V + O X-ray vision to slice through long sentences fast.

✍️ Writing

Task 1 magic pair: swap Verb + Adverb (rose sharply) with Adjective + Noun (a sharp rise) to boost variety! Save 3 min at the end to check S+V · -s · articles.

🗣️ Speaking

Examiners reward error-free simple sentences + natural complex clauses: link two clean core patterns with because, although, when, or if!

Final workout — IELTS Gap-Fill X-Ray / 品詞で解く実践ドリル
